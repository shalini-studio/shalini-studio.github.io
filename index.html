परिचय · About

Shalini Singh

Founder & Principal — Interior Designer & Architect

Shalini Singh founded Prashree Architects with a belief that every space should tell a story. Drawing inspiration from India's rich architectural legacy — the carved jharokhas of Rajasthan, the courtyards of Kerala homes, the geometry of Mughal gardens — she creates spaces that feel rooted yet refreshingly modern.

From intimate homes to boutique commercial projects, the studio's work balances natural materials, handcrafted details and thoughtful light to create environments that are as functional as they are beautiful.
